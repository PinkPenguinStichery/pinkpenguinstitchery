About Leslie

Where every stitch
tells a story

"Stitching is how I slow down, create something lasting, and share a little love with the world."

Hi, I'm Leslie — wife, mom of three, and the maker behind Pink Penguin Stitchery. Family is everything to me, and when I'm not busy with the chaos and joy that comes with three kids, you'll find me at my embroidery hoop, designing and stitching custom patterns.

I started Pink Penguin Stitchery in 2026 to share my love of the craft with others. Every pattern I design is made with care — whether it's a personalized gift, a keepsake, or just something beautiful to stitch on a quiet evening.
